
# LingoVibe 🌍🎵

Welcome to LingoVibe!

LingoVibe is a multilingual platform designed to help people understand music and videos in different languages.

## Main Features

- Music translation
- Video translation
- Multilingual subtitles
- User accounts
- Free and Premium plans

## Our Mission

To break language barriers and connect people around the world through music and video.
